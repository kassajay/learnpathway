1. Georgia Tech Online Master of Science in Computer Science

Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS) is designed for students who want a graduate Computer Science degree while maintaining flexibility around work and other responsibilities.

Why consider Georgia Tech OMSCS?

  • Online graduate Computer Science degree
  • Coursework and activities are supplied asynchronously
  • Students can take one course per semester
  • The program allows up to six years to complete the degree
  • Applications are accepted for Fall and Spring

Admissions

Georgia Tech lists a bachelor's degree or equivalent as a requirement. Preferred qualifications include an undergraduate degree in Computer Science or a related field such as mathematics, computer engineering or electrical engineering, with a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher.

International applicants may also need to provide satisfactory TOEFL or IELTS-Academic scores, depending on the applicable requirements.

Application Deadlines

  • Fall: March 1
  • Spring: August 15
  • No Summer admissions are listed by the program.

Admission is competitive, and meeting minimum requirements does not guarantee admission.

2. UT Austin Online Master's in Computer Science

The University of Texas at Austin offers an online Master of Science in Computer Science designed to develop advanced Computer Science skills that can be applied across technology, finance, healthcare and other industries.

Program Structure

  • 30 total credit hours
  • 10 courses
  • 3 required courses
  • 7 elective courses
  • Elective areas include systems, programming languages, machine learning and more

Tuition

UT Austin currently lists the online master's program at approximately $10,000 plus applicable fees. The university notes that additional fees may apply in some situations.

Admissions

Applicants are generally required to have a bachelor's degree in a related field from a regionally accredited institution or a comparable foreign academic institution.

The program lists a minimum GPA of 3.0 in upper-division coursework and completed graduate work.

Application Deadlines

  • Fall opens: December 15
  • Fall priority: March 15
  • Fall final: April 15
  • Spring opens: June 1
  • Spring priority: August 1
  • Spring final: September 1

Who Should Consider an Online CS Master's?

An online Computer Science master's degree may be worth considering if you already have a bachelor's degree and want to deepen your technical knowledge without attending a traditional full-time campus program.

  • Software developers who want advanced technical skills
  • IT professionals moving toward software or engineering roles
  • Professionals interested in artificial intelligence or machine learning
  • Students interested in systems, algorithms or programming languages
  • Career changers with an appropriate technical background
  • Professionals who need a flexible study schedule

What to Check Before Applying

  • Does the program match your career goal?
  • Do you meet the academic requirements?
  • Do you have the required Computer Science background?
  • Can you manage the weekly study workload?
  • Have you checked the current tuition and fees?
  • Have you checked the latest application deadline?
  • Do you understand international student requirements if applicable?
  • Have you prepared transcripts and recommendation materials?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I complete a Computer Science master's degree online?

Yes. Universities such as Georgia Tech and UT Austin offer online Computer Science master's programs.

Is an online CS master's the same as a coding bootcamp?

No. A master's degree is a graduate academic degree, while a coding bootcamp is generally a shorter skills-focused training program.

Do I need a Computer Science bachelor's degree?

Requirements vary by university. Georgia Tech prefers applicants with a Computer Science or related undergraduate degree, while UT Austin says applicants without a related bachelor's degree may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Can international students apply?

International applicants can apply to these online programs, but requirements such as academic equivalency and English-language proficiency can apply. Always check the official university requirements.

Should I apply immediately?

Before applying, compare the curriculum, total cost, deadlines, admissions requirements and workload. Then use the university's official application website.

Final Thoughts

An online Computer Science master's degree can be a flexible way to build advanced technical knowledge while continuing your professional or personal commitments.

Georgia Tech and UT Austin are two strong options to research, but the best program for you depends on your background, goals, budget and preferred learning style.

Always verify tuition, deadlines, eligibility and application requirements directly with the university before submitting an application.
