Privacy Policy for LearnPathway

At LearnPathway, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information that may be collected when you use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what types of information may be collected, how that information may be used, and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Information We May Collect

LearnPathway may collect information automatically when visitors access or interact with the website.

Information Provided by Visitors

If you voluntarily contact us, you may provide information such as your name, email address, or the contents of your message.

Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, LearnPathway may receive technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location, referring pages, and information about how visitors interact with the website.

2. How We May Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate website and communication purposes, including:

  • Responding to questions or messages
  • Improving website content and user experience
  • Understanding how visitors use the website
  • Maintaining website security
  • Identifying technical problems
  • Improving educational resources and guides

3. Cookies

LearnPathway and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or provide relevant advertising.

Cookies are small files stored on a visitor's device by a website. They may help websites remember preferences and understand visitor interactions.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

LearnPathway may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to display advertisements on the website.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this or other websites.

Visitors may have options to manage personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings and other available privacy controls.

Advertising technologies and available controls may change over time. Visitors should review the relevant third-party privacy information for the latest details.

5. Third-Party Links

LearnPathway may contain links to external websites, universities, educational platforms, and other third-party resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of external websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policy of any external website they choose to visit.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with LearnPathway. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

7. Children's Privacy

LearnPathway is an educational information website. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or purchasing products.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate steps can be considered.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain uses of your information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact LearnPathway using the contact information provided on our Contact page.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, advertising partners, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information may be handled on LearnPathway, please visit our Contact page.
