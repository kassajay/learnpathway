Looking for the right online MBA or business program?

An MBA can help students develop knowledge in areas such as leadership, finance, marketing, operations, strategy and business analytics.

Online business education has also expanded beyond traditional MBA degrees. Students can choose specialized master's degrees, professional certificates and individual business courses.

This LearnPathway guide explains the major options so you can compare programs before applying or paying tuition.

What Is an MBA?

MBA stands for Master of Business Administration. It is a graduate-level business degree designed to develop knowledge and skills in management, leadership and business decision-making.

MBA programs commonly cover subjects such as accounting, finance, marketing, operations, strategy, economics, organizational behavior and leadership.

Many online MBA programs are designed for working professionals, allowing students to study remotely while continuing their careers.

Who Should Consider an Online MBA?

👔 Working Professionals

Professionals can use an MBA to build management knowledge while continuing to work.

🚀 Future Managers

People preparing for leadership roles can develop skills in strategy, communication and decision-making.

💼 Entrepreneurs

Business owners and aspiring entrepreneurs can study finance, marketing, operations and business strategy.

🔄 Career Changers

An MBA can provide broad business education for professionals moving into a different business function or industry.

What Do You Learn in an MBA?

Curriculum varies by university, but common MBA subjects include:

📊 Finance

Learn financial decision-making, corporate finance, budgeting and financial analysis.

📣 Marketing

Study customer behavior, marketing strategy, branding, digital marketing and market analysis.

🧠 Strategy

Learn how organizations develop competitive strategies and make long-term business decisions.

👥 Leadership

Develop skills related to leadership, teamwork, organizational behavior and communication.

⚙️ Operations

Explore supply chains, operations management, productivity and organizational processes.

📈 Business Analytics

Learn how data and analytical methods can support business decisions.

Online MBA Programs to Explore

The following university programs are examples of online MBA options. Always check the official university website for current tuition, deadlines and admission requirements.

Arizona State University — W. P. Carey Online MBA

Arizona State University's W. P. Carey School of Business offers a 100% online MBA designed for working professionals.

The current program information lists 18 classes and 51 credit hours. The program does not require GMAT or GRE scores for admission.

ASU also states that the program offers multiple start periods and allows students to customize their studies with concentrations and electives.

Current program information: ASU currently lists the Online MBA as AACSB-accredited, with no GMAT/GRE requirement and a flexible online format.

How Much Does an Online MBA Cost?

Online MBA costs vary significantly by university. Tuition may depend on the number of credits, program structure, residency status and other university fees.

🎓 Tuition

Compare the total estimated tuition rather than only the advertised per-credit price.

📚 Course Materials

Consider textbooks, software and other required learning materials.

💳 Application Fees

Some universities charge application or enrollment fees.

🎓 Financial Aid

Check scholarships, employer tuition assistance and other available financial aid options.

Example: ASU's current official Online MBA page lists an estimated total program cost of $74,000 for resident, nonresident and international students. Tuition can change, so verify the current amount directly with ASU before applying.

Common Online MBA Admission Requirements

Every university has its own admissions process. Common requirements may include:

🎓 Bachelor's Degree

Many MBA programs require applicants to have completed an undergraduate degree.

📄 Transcripts

Universities generally request academic records from previous institutions.

💼 Resume

Applicants may need to provide professional and educational background information.

📝 Essays

Some MBA programs ask applicants to explain their goals, experience and reasons for pursuing an MBA.

👥 Recommendations

Some programs require professional or academic recommendation letters.

🌎 English Proficiency

International applicants may need to provide proof of English proficiency depending on the university.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an online MBA worth it?

An online MBA can be valuable when the program's curriculum, cost, flexibility and career resources align with your goals. The value depends on the individual program and student.

Can I get an MBA without a business degree?

Some MBA programs accept students from a wide range of undergraduate backgrounds. Always check the specific university's admission requirements.

Can I study an MBA while working?

Many online MBA programs are designed for working professionals. However, workload and scheduling vary by university.

Do online MBA programs require the GMAT?

Requirements vary. Some online MBA programs do not require GMAT or GRE scores, while others may have different standardized-test policies.

Is an MBA useful for entrepreneurs?

MBA coursework can provide useful knowledge in finance, strategy, marketing, operations and leadership. However, entrepreneurship also requires practical experience and business execution.

Can I learn business without getting an MBA?

Yes. Professional certificates, individual courses, specialized master's programs and practical experience are alternative ways to develop business skills.

How long does an online MBA take?

Completion time varies by program and study pace. Some online MBA programs can be completed in roughly two years, while flexible programs may allow a longer completion period.

Final Thoughts

An online MBA can be a flexible way to develop business, management and leadership knowledge while continuing your career.

However, an MBA is not automatically the best option for everyone. A specialized master's degree, professional certificate or individual course may be more appropriate depending on your career goal.

Before applying, compare the curriculum, total cost, accreditation, admissions requirements, flexibility and career support offered by each program.

Important: Tuition, deadlines, program structures and admission requirements can change. Always verify current information directly with the university before making an enrollment or financial decision.
